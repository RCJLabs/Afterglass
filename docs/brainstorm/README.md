# Five Keeps: concept brainstorm (September 2026)

Open [`index.html`](index.html) in a browser for the full version: animated pixel-art mockups of each concept, the competitor table, an adjustable weighted comparison, and the decision list.

**Round two:** Deadhold and Mirrorhold were chosen as finalists. Ten follow-up ideas are in [`round-2/`](round-2/README.md).

## The problem with the pitch

"Fallout Shelter–style side view, Elder Scrolls–style fantasy, build and defend a castle" already exists twice on Google Play:

- **The Elder Scrolls: Castles** (Bethesda Game Studios, global launch Sep 2024): Fallout Shelter in a Tamriel castle. It has **no castle defense**; fights happen away from the castle.
- **Hustle Castle** (MY.GAMES, 2017): a side-view medieval Fallout Shelter with PvE invasions and PvP raids.

Craft the World, Kingdom Two Crowns (Kingdom III due 2027), Hammerting, Grow Castle and Castle Doombad cover most of the rest. Setting and camera won't differentiate the game. A defense mechanic that only works in cross-section can.

## Recommendation

**Hoardkeep, with Countermine's tunnel war as its defense.** You build on a sleeping dragon, and everything makes noise: your forges, your gold-skimming, and the enemy's tunnels. You defend by listening for their digging. Your own noise risks waking the dragon, and waking it on purpose is your last-resort weapon. PC/Steam-first instead: build pure **Countermine**.

## The five concepts

| Concept | Hook | Novelty (per search) | Scope | Best fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Countermine | Hold the walls; detect and intercept the sappers tunnelling under them | Strong: no game found built around it | Medium | PC / web, landscape, roguelite runs |
| **Hoardkeep** | Build on a sleeping dragon; noise raises its stir; waking it is a weapon | Good: the combination looks new | Medium | Phone, portrait, persistent keep |
| Mirrorhold | Every room has an upside-down twin in a shadow realm; day/night alternate | Strong: closest is a small itch.io game | Medium–large | Phone, portrait |
| Hollow Colossus | Settle inside a dormant brass giant; its guardians are an immune system; make it walk | Moderate: Ark of Charon, The Wandering Village nearby | Large | Phone, portrait |
| Deadhold | Every attacker you kill becomes a worker | Low: Necromancer Accountant, NecroCity | Medium | Either |

Working titles are placeholders. "Veilkeep" was dropped because Veilkeeper exists; Mirrorhold and Deadhold weren't checked.

## Next two weeks

1. **Week 1, Hoardkeep greybox.** One mountain, five rooms, the dragon as a box with a breathing timer and a stir meter, noise spread by BFS through the room graph, one attacker type, one enemy sap, a wake button. *Kill if* the best play is "never run the forge", or waking is always right or always wrong.
2. **Week 2, Countermine greybox.** One wall, a 40×12 underground grid with fog of war, saps that path toward the foundations, listening posts that return direction and distance. *Kill if* detection becomes a fixed sweep pattern.
