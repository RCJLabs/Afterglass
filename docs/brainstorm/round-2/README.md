# Bone & Glass: round two (September 2026)

Ten concepts grown from the two round-one finalists, Deadhold and Mirrorhold. Open [`index.html`](index.html) in a browser for the animated mockups. Each idea is drawn in a different art style, and the page also has an interface wireframe, loop diagrams, a storyboard, store art, a nine-style comparison of one scene, and an adjustable scoring table.

## Before you commit

The undead-worker space got more crowded this week. *Graveyard Keeper 2* shipped on 22 Sep 2026 with zombie workers, and *Sintopia* (Apr 2026) drops the dead into a layer you manage below the surface. Pure Deadhold ideas now need a strong twist; the fusions get their novelty from the mirror structure. Six of the first working titles were taken or too close to existing games and have been renamed.

## Recommendation

**Prototype Barrowdeep first.** It's the highest-scoring idea that uses both halves: every body you bury grows a room in the mirrored world below. Tombwright scores highest overall but is a tomb rather than a castle and uses only the Deadhold half.

## Name status

Checked clean: Tombwright, Hourglass Keep. Unchecked: the other eight. Dropped because taken or too close: Glasswake, Undercroft, Ossuary, Harrow, Graveyard Shift, Tidekeep, Shadowcast, Sinistral.
