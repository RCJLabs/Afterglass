A base-builder in development

Afterglass

You hold a keep on the Veil. By day the living build, craft and hold the walls. Anyone who dies inside them wakes at dusk as a shade and works the night shift in the reflected keep. At dawn you decide which of the dead stay.

Play AfterglassIn the browser, on a phone or a computer. To install it as an app, open it and choose Install on its main screen.

Play the prototypes

Each prototype tests a single question from the plan. The first two are greyboxes, the third is the first art, and the fourth joins them into one season.

  • Weeks 7–10 sliceOne seasonWould you play a second season? Seven days and nights in a keep that starts as two rooms and is built up a room at a time: escalating raids, Maws that break whatever you leave bare, the Lantern Church's first inspection, and the Hollow on the new moon. Starts with an optional guide; installs as an app and plays offline.
  • Weeks 1–2 greyboxTwo rosters and the RiteDoes a death feel like a loss and a hire at once?
  • Weeks 3–4 greyboxThe night on its ownIs the night fun without the day? Candles, Creepers, fading and remembrance.
  • Weeks 5–6 pixel passFirst artCan players read the Tain on a phone? Four room pairs, the dusk crossing, two night cameras and a timed tap test.
